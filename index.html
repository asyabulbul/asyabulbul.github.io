Asya Bülbül

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Government at Uppsala University. I am a political psychologist studying voter–party dynamics, affective polarization, and political representation. I use methods ranging from integrative social science genomics to mixed-methods designs and survey experiments. I am also a part of the European Social Science Genetics Network (ESSGN) as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie PhD candidate.
